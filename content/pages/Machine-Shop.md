## Machine Shop

## Precision Machining, Delivered with Confidence

Machining is an integral part of our aluminium casting workflow. Our in-house CNC and conventional facilities support milling, turning, drilling and secondary operations, helping take components from cast form to finished parts. With equipment for both development work and repeat production, we provide practical machining support at every stage.

### CNC Milling & Turning

Our VMC and CNC turning centres handle milling and turning work for cast components, with machine capacities suited to a range of part sizes.

### Conventional Machining Support

Lathe, radial drilling and drill machines provide flexible support for prototypes, small batches and secondary machining operations.

### CNC Capability

- ACE-450 VMC — X axis 800 mm, Y axis 450 mm, Z axis 550 mm
- ACE Turning Centre with 250 mm chuck capacity
- Jyoti Turning Centre with 250 mm chuck capacity
- Efficient machining support for repeat production and close-tolerance components

### Conventional Machining Support

- Flexible support for prototypes, small batches and secondary operations
- Lathe machines up to 500 mm centre height
- All-geared radial drilling machines up to 50 mm drilling capacity
- Drill machines up to 1 inch diameter capacity
