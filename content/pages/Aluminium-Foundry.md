## Aluminium Foundry

## Aluminium Casting Expertise, From Melt to Finished Casting

Since 1982, Alert Engineering Works has manufactured aluminium alloy castings using gravity die casting and sand casting processes. We support one-off development work through to larger production batches, with casting weights from 10 g up to 465 kg per piece.

### Organised Foundry Floor

A clean, organised production environment supports safe workflows and dependable foundry operations from batch to batch.

### Casting Processes & Production Range

- Gravity die casting and sand casting in a range of aluminium alloys
- Batch sizes from one-off castings to larger production quantities
- Single-piece casting weights from 10 g up to 465 kg
- Current production and developed castings, including special aluminium-tin alloy grades

### Melt Quality & Process Control

Our foundry uses gas melting furnaces. Aluminium melt quality is checked using rotary degassing, a vacuum test system and a thermal analysis system, with check results recorded in the relevant software.

### Aluminium Alloy Grades

**BS 1490:** LM4, LM5, LM6, LM9, LM13, LM16, LM25

**ASTM:** 204, 208, 319, 154, A413, A360, 336, 355, A356, 356

**DIN:** AlSiC4, AlMg5, AlSi12, AlSi10Mg, AlSi12Cu, AlSi5Cu1Mg, AlSi7Mg

**Special alloy:** ASTM B850 (tin alloy with Si – 0.4% max and Tin 5.5–7%)
