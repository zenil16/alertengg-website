## Quality Policy

## Quality Built into Every Casting

At Alert Engineering Works, quality guides how we manufacture pressure, gravity and sand-cast components, dies and patterns for sand castings. We are committed to meeting customer requirements and providing prompt service through capable people, controlled processes and continual improvement.

### Our Aim

Supply sound, blowhole-free castings that meet agreed customer requirements.

### Our Vision

Be the best die caster through dependable quality and customer-focused service.

### Continual Improvement

Improve products, processes and systems, adopt appropriate technology, and strengthen employee skills through regular training.

## Our Quality Objectives

We track these measurable objectives to support customer satisfaction.

| No. | Quality Objective | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | On-time delivery | 95% |
| 2 | Overall rejection (customer complaints and internal rejection) | 01% |
| 3 | Respond to customer complaints within 24 working hours | 100% |

We review our quality objectives and work together to improve the consistency of our products, processes and service.
